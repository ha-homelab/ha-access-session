# Governance and continuity

The primary maintainer, `4alvit`, owns project direction, review, security response and release acceptance. Contributors propose issues and pull requests. Reviewers assess implementation, tests, deployment assumptions and compatibility; automated checks supply evidence and do not authorize a live deployment.

The maintainer confirmed knowledge of secure design and common Python/network application vulnerabilities and prevention. No external bug reports, feature requests or vulnerability reports were received in the preceding twelve months as of 2026-10-16. This is a dated observation, not a promise about future reports.

There is no backup person or tested arrangement that can restore repository, CI and release access within one week. Bot access and public source do not establish continuity. Silver access continuity and a bus factor of two remain unmet until a real arrangement is established and tested.

Use CONTRIBUTING.md, CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md, SECURITY.md and RELEASING.md for their respective processes. Discuss scope and priorities through public issues, documenting reasons and compatibility effects in reviews.
