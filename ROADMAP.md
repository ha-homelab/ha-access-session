# Roadmap

This roadmap covers October 2026 through October 2027.

- Preserve fixed-origin verification, explicit administrator mapping and bounded revocation semantics.
- Maintain current Home Assistant compatibility and negative authentication/TLS tests.
- Audit runtime and release-tool dependencies and update verified toolchains promptly.
- Qualify the complete HTTPS deployment and document its operational assumptions.
- Improve coverage, accessibility and release reproducibility with measured evidence.
- Establish authorized, tested maintainer access continuity.

These are plans, not completed properties. Automatic cloud-policy changes, implicit trust of network headers, unrestricted proxying and undocumented expansion of administrator access are not goals.
