# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexualized conduct, repeated unwanted contact and disclosure of private information are unacceptable in project spaces or while representing the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to **alvit.work@gmail.com**, including relevant links and only information needed to investigate. The primary maintainer aims to acknowledge within 14 days, protects identifying information where possible, and may remove content, warn, restrict participation or exclude participants in proportion to severity and repetition. Retaliation is prohibited.

A request to reconsider a decision may use the same address. There is currently no independent moderator or appeal panel; a complaint about the primary maintainer will reach that maintainer. GitHub's platform reporting process is also available. Technical vulnerabilities use SECURITY.md.
